About Me

I am a graduate of the Applied Bachelor of Electrical Engineering program at Manado State Polytechnic. My academic interests include electrical power systems, renewable energy, battery protection, load shedding, PLC-based automation, ESP32, and IoT monitoring.

I enjoy developing practical engineering systems that combine power electronics, protection, control, sensing, and digital monitoring. My recent work has focused on battery protection for hybrid renewable-energy systems and IoT-based monitoring of electrical protection systems.
